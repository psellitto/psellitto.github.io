### Master
* **Aérosols et Climat** (Master M2 SGE Re)
  * **Objectives**:
    - Introduce the Earth's climate system:
      - The Atmosphere, the Ocean, the Biosphere and their interactions
      - (Bio)geochemical cycles
      - Climate trends of the past and present
    - Understand the role of aerosols in the climate system as a function of their sources, processes and properties
    - Discuss of the international climate governance and future scanarios  
  * **Structure**: 5 courses x 3h each: 15h overall
  * **Evaluation**: one oral presentation on a selected subject (50%) + one written exam (50%)
